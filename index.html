Rules

One sentence at a time. Lists, settings and forms wait in sheets.
The sentence card keeps its height, so Play and Got it never move.
Red acts. Play, Got it, pinyin and what you are on. Characters stay ink.
Serif for the Chinese you read. Outfit for everything else. 说 in the name uses the same sans as the icon.
Destructive actions always go last. A single one is plain coral text, centered. Two or more related ones are grouped in one card. They always ask first.
Got it moves, never deletes. Delete lives in Edit sentence, and it always asks first.
Top left leaves a sheet. × closes it, ‹ goes back one step. The bottom holds one forward action.
Cards only for controls and lists. White with a 1px line and radius 16. No shadows.
Every round button is one flat grey circle, 44 wide, with a 20 icon. Disabled only fades the icon.
Five text styles, like iOS. 34, 20, 17 semibold, 17 regular, 13 regular. The same in all three apps.
