# PlayControls

The main action, with previous and next on either side.

- Play is `primary`, `size-play` tall, `radius-pill`, with an icon and the word Play. While playing it reads Pause with a pause icon.
- Previous and next are the family round button. Flat `chip` circle, 44 wide, a 20 chevron with a 2 line in `ink`, no shadow.
- At the first or last sentence the matching button keeps its circle, its icon fades to `chevron`, and it does nothing.
- Moving while playing keeps playing from the new sentence.
