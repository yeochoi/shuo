# SheetBar

The top of every sheet.

- Three columns. A round `size-nav-button` on `line` at the left, the title in `title` in the middle, the right left empty.
- × closes a first screen. ‹ goes back one step, like Design system back to Settings. Tapping the dimmed page around a sheet closes it too.
- Titles say what the sheet is, like Sentences, New sentence, Settings.
